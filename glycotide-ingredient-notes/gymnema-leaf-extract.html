About Gymnema Leaf Extract

In short: A traditional herb linked to sugar taste and glucose.*

Gymnema sylvestre is a woody vine used for centuries in traditional Indian practice, and it is studied for its effect on sweet taste and blood glucose. The seller names it first and ties it to sweet cravings.

See the ingredients and safety and medicine notes.

A Traditional Vine

Gymnema has a long history in Indian medicine. See gymnema leaf extract: what to know.

Why It Needs Care

It can lower blood sugar, so it matters with diabetes medicine. See safety and medicine notes.

Main Caution

Gymnema can lower blood sugar, which matters for anyone on diabetes medicine. Ask a doctor and pharmacist first and keep monitoring as advised. See safety and medicine notes.
