BHB Calcium in Jellutide: Plain Facts

By the Jellutide Editorial Team · Updated October 27, 2026

BHB Calcium is one of the ingredients the seller names for Jellutide (beta-hydroxybutyrate calcium salt); part of the 525 mg proprietary blend; the individual amount is not disclosed. BHB Calcium is beta-hydroxybutyrate, a molecule the body makes, bound to calcium to form a salt.

What BHB Calcium Is in Jellutide

BHB Calcium is beta-hydroxybutyrate, a molecule the body makes, bound to calcium to form a salt.

Where the BHB Calcium in Jellutide Comes From

It is made in a lab by combining BHB with calcium.

Why BHB Calcium Is in the Jellutide Formula

It is one of three BHB salts in the blend, with the declared calcium only 1 mg per gummy, and no treatment claim attached. Jellutide is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for memory loss or any condition, and no result is promised.

Ask Your Doctor About BHB Calcium in Jellutide If

You take calcium supplements, kidney stone medicine or heart medicine, or monitor calcium intake. The declared amount is tiny, so the gummy is not a meaningful calcium source. Safe use of dietary supplements: NIH NCCIH.
