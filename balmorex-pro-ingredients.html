Balmorex Pro Ingredients: Arnica, Aloe, MSM, Menthol and More

The eleven named ingredients, and the main caution for each.

The seller names eleven ingredients: sunflower oil, shea butter, arnica oil, aloe vera, bromelain, magnesium sulfate, MSM, niacin, ginger root extract, menthol and hemp seed oil, with no amounts.* The seller's text also mentions frankincense, which is not on its list, and a "27-in-1" figure that cannot be confirmed. Each note covers what the item is, what to know and who should be careful, and the home page has a tap-through version.

Sunflower Oil in Balmorex Pro

In short: A light plant oil that carries the formula.*

Sunflower oil is a common skin-friendly carrier oil rich in vitamin E, and the seller describes it as an antioxidant-rich base. It helps the cream spread without feeling heavy.

More on sunflower oil · safety and patch test

Shea Butter in Balmorex Pro

In short: A rich plant butter used in moisturisers.*

Shea butter comes from the nut of the African shea tree and is a classic moisturiser, with vitamins A and E and fatty acids.

More on shea butter · safety and patch test

Arnica Oil in Balmorex Pro

In short: A traditional herb for bruises and sore muscles.*

Arnica (Arnica montana) is a mountain daisy that has long been used in rubs for sore muscles and bruising. The seller lists arnica oil among its soothing ingredients.

More on arnica oil · safety and patch test

Aloe Vera in Balmorex Pro

In short: A familiar soothing plant gel.*

Aloe vera gel is widely used on skin to soothe and hydrate, and the seller says it contains compounds that could reduce discomfort.

More on aloe vera · safety and patch test

Bromelain in Balmorex Pro

In short: An enzyme from pineapple.*

Bromelain is a mixture of enzymes from pineapple, taken by mouth for swelling in some studies. The seller lists it as a soothing ingredient for sprains and strains.

More on bromelain · safety and patch test

Magnesium Sulfate in Balmorex Pro

In short: Better known as Epsom salt.*

Magnesium sulfate is Epsom salt, long used in baths for tired muscles. The seller says it plays a part in how pain is sensed.

More on magnesium sulfate · safety and patch test

MSM in Balmorex Pro

In short: A sulfur compound found in plants and foods.*

MSM (methylsulfonylmethane) is a sulfur compound sold for joint comfort, mostly as a supplement taken by mouth, and the seller lists it as a natural joint comfort ingredient.

More on msm · safety and patch test

Niacin in Balmorex Pro

In short: Vitamin B3.*

Niacin (vitamin B3) is a vitamin, and in topical products it is sometimes included for a warming sensation on the skin.

More on niacin · safety and patch test

Ginger Root Extract in Balmorex Pro

In short: A warming root used in food and tea.*

Ginger root extract carries natural compounds such as gingerols, and the seller describes it as soothing.

More on ginger root extract · safety and patch test

Menthol in Balmorex Pro

In short: The cooling compound from mint.*

Menthol is the cooling compound in peppermint, used for centuries in rubs because it creates a cool feeling that distracts from aches. The seller notes its long use.

More on menthol · safety and patch test

Hemp Seed Oil in Balmorex Pro

In short: A nourishing oil pressed from hemp seeds.*

Hemp seed oil is pressed from the seeds of the hemp plant and is rich in fatty acids, and it is used in moisturisers. The seller lists it as a well-known topical ingredient.

More on hemp seed oil · safety and patch test
