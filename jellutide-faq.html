Jellutide Questions and Answers

What is Jellutide?

A berry-flavored dietary supplement gummy for adults, 30 per bottle, with a 525 mg blend of apple cider vinegar and BHB calcium, magnesium and sodium salts. See about Jellutide.

What does the label say per gummy?

Calories 8, total carbohydrate 2 g, total sugars 2 g (1 g added), sodium 2.5 mg, calcium 1 mg, magnesium 0.75 mg and a 525 mg proprietary blend. See the Jellutide ingredients.

Are the vinegar and BHB amounts listed?

No. The blend totals 525 mg, but the individual amounts of apple cider vinegar and each BHB salt are not disclosed.

What are the other ingredients?

Corn syrup, purified water, cane sugar, apple pectin, citric acid, sodium citrate, tapioca starch, natural flavors and beet root powder.

How do I take Jellutide?

One gummy before a meal, as the label directs, never more. See how Jellutide works.

Will Jellutide make me lose weight?

No result is promised. The research on vinegar and BHB was not done on this gummy, and it is not a stand-alone weight-loss solution. See what weight management gummies cannot do.

Is Jellutide sugar-free?

No. Each gummy has 2 g of sugars (1 g added) and 8 calories. See sugar and calories in a gummy.

Is it safe with my medicines?

Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, especially about insulin, diabetes, digoxin and water-pill medicine. See apple cider vinegar and your medicines.

Who should not take it?

Anyone under 18, anyone pregnant or nursing, and anyone on the medicines above, or watching sugar, sodium, calcium or magnesium, who has not asked a doctor first.

How long does a bottle last?

One gummy a day from 30 gummies is 30 days, so two bottles last about 60 days, three about 90 and six about 180.

A 60-day money-back guarantee from the purchase date is advertised. Contact support with your order number for return instructions. See the 60-day guarantee page.
