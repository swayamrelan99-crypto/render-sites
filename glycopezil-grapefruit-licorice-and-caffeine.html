Grapefruit, Licorice, Caffeine And Other Cautions

A short list for your pharmacist.*

Grapefruit Extract

Grapefruit can change how some medicines are processed, including some statins and blood pressure drugs. The amount here is not stated, so ask a pharmacist.

See also: Diabetes medicines and low sugar · Kidney, liver, heart conditions

Licorice-Derived Glycyrrhizinate

Licorice compounds can raise blood pressure and lower potassium.

See also: Kidney, liver, heart conditions · FAQ

Caffeine And Stimulants

Guarana and green tea carry caffeine, and capsicum and coleus can raise heart rate or change blood pressure.

See also: FAQ · Green Tea, EGCG And Blood Sugar

Amino Acids

L-tryptophan can interact with antidepressants, L-carnitine with warfarin, L-arginine with blood pressure and nitrate medicines, and L-tyrosine with thyroid medicine.
