Chest Pain And Urgent Signs: Skip The Supplement

If you read one safety page, read this one.*

Call Emergency Services For

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness, especially with exertion
  • Shortness of breath, fainting or a racing, irregular heartbeat
  • Sudden weakness or numbness on one side, a drooping face or trouble speaking
  • An erection lasting more than four hours

See also: Low energy: find the cause · Medicines and interactions

See A Doctor Soon For

  • Falling energy that lasts weeks, or loud snoring with daytime sleepiness
  • Low mood, loss of interest or changes in drive that persist
  • Persistent erection or performance problems, which can be an early sign of heart or blood-vessel disease
  • Unexplained weight loss, night sweats or breast changes

See also: Medicines and interactions · FAQ

Why

These can point to heart disease, sleep apnea, diabetes, depression, thyroid or hormone problems, all of which have specific treatments. A supplement cannot assess them.
