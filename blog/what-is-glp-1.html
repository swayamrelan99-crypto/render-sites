What Is GLP-1, and What Does a "Natural Booster" Mean?

GLP-1 comes up constantly in weight and blood sugar conversations lately. Here's what it actually is.

The basic science

GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) is a hormone the body naturally produces that plays a role in insulin release and appetite regulation.

Prescription GLP-1 medications

Some prescription medications work by mimicking or boosting GLP-1 activity directly, under a doctor's supervision.

What a "natural GLP-1 booster" supplement means

Products like GLPro are positioned as supporting the body's own processes through ingredients like Berberine and Cinnamon Bark, not as a replacement for prescription GLP-1 medication. Always talk to your doctor about what's right for your situation.
