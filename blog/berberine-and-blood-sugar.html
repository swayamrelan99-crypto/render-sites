Berberine and Blood Sugar: What the Research Says

Berberine is the first ingredient listed in GLPro's formula, and one of the most studied plant compounds in the blood sugar category.

What is Berberine?

Berberine is a compound found in several plants, including barberry and goldenseal, used for centuries in traditional medicine.

What research has explored

Modern research has studied Berberine's connection to glucose metabolism and insulin response, often comparing it informally to common blood sugar medications.
