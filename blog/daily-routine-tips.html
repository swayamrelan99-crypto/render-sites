5 Tips for Building a Daily Blood Sugar Routine

The best supplement routine is the one you actually stick with. Here are five practical tips for a daily blood sugar routine.

1. Anchor it to an existing habit

Tie your capsule to something you already do every day — breakfast, brushing your teeth, or your morning coffee.

2. Keep it visible

Keep your bottle somewhere you'll see it at the right time of day.

3. Take it with a full glass of water

GLPro's label suggests taking it with water — make that part of the routine, not an afterthought.

4. Track it loosely

A simple calendar checkmark is enough to build a visible pattern.

5. Give it real time

Daily wellness habits work gradually. Give any new routine several weeks before judging results.
