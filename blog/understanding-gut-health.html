Understanding Gut Health

"Gut health" gets used a lot, but what does it actually refer to? Here's a grounded starting point.

More Than Just Digestion

Gut health encompasses the balance of bacteria in your digestive tract, the integrity of your intestinal lining, and how efficiently your body breaks down and absorbs nutrients — not just whether you feel bloated after a meal.

Why It Connects To So Much Else

The gut processes everything you consume and plays a significant role in immune function, which is part of why gut health conversations often extend into energy, mood and overall wellness.

Signs Worth Paying Attention To

Bloating, irregular digestion, and feeling unusually fatigued after meals are common signals that gut health may be worth a closer look.
