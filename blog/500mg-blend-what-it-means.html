What A "500mg Blend" Really Tells You

BloodPril names all three of its ingredients — but only discloses their combined weight, not the individual split. Here's what that structure actually means.

What You Do Know

A 500mg blend with three named ingredients tells you the maximum possible total — none of the three can individually exceed roughly 500mg, and together they sum to that figure.

What You Don't Know

You don't know whether the blend is roughly even across all three, or whether one ingredient dominates and the other two are present in trace amounts. Order on a label is typically by descending weight, but that's a convention, not a guarantee.

Why This Matters For Comparing To Research

Most clinical research on L-Arginine or L-Citrulline studies specific doses, often in the multiple grams per day. Without knowing the actual split inside a 500mg blend, you can't directly compare BloodPril's formula to that research.

The Honest Bottom Line

This isn't unique to BloodPril — it's standard practice across much of the supplement industry. The difference worth noting is whether a brand says so plainly, which is why we flag it directly rather than letting it pass unmentioned.
