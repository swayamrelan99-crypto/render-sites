Probiotics & Microbiome Balance

Multi-Strain Probiotics anchor GutSave's formula, delivering 50 billion CFU across 12 targeted strains.

Why Strain Count And CFU Both Matter

A probiotic's effectiveness depends on both the number of live organisms (CFU, or colony-forming units) and the diversity of strains included — GutSave combines 12 strains at 50 billion CFU rather than relying on a single strain.

What Beneficial Bacteria Actually Do

Beneficial gut bacteria help break down food, produce certain vitamins, and compete with less helpful microbes for space and resources in the digestive tract.

Why Probiotics Alone Aren't The Whole Story

Probiotics introduce beneficial bacteria, but those bacteria still need the right environment to thrive — which is why GutSave pairs probiotics with prebiotic fiber. See our full ingredient breakdown for how the two work together.
