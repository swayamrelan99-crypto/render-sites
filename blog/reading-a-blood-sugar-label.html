How to Read a Blood Sugar Supplement Label

Blood sugar supplement labels can vary a lot between brands. Here's what to look for.

Named ingredients vs. proprietary blends

Some labels, like GLPro's, name each ingredient individually. Others combine several into an unlabeled "proprietary blend," which makes it harder to know exact amounts.

Serving size and directions

Check how many capsules make up a serving and how often you're meant to take them — GLPro, for example, directs one capsule daily.

Why this matters

Understanding a label helps you compare products honestly. See GLPro's full label for an example.
