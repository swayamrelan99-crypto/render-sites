Microbiome Basics, Explained

The microbiome is one of the most talked-about topics in gut health — here's a grounded explanation of what it actually is.

What The Microbiome Is

Your gut microbiome is the community of trillions of bacteria and other microorganisms living in your digestive tract, with hundreds of different species represented in a healthy gut.

Why Diversity Matters

A more diverse microbiome, with many different beneficial species represented, is generally associated with better digestive resilience than a microbiome dominated by just a few strains.

What Disrupts It

Diet, stress, certain medications, and lack of sleep can all shift the balance of your microbiome, sometimes in ways that take time to restore.

The Probiotic-Prebiotic Connection

Restoring microbiome balance generally benefits from both reintroducing beneficial bacteria (probiotics) and feeding them (prebiotics) — see our probiotics piece for more detail.
