Nitric Oxide & Blood Pressure: The Research

The nitric oxide pathway isn't a marketing invention — it's genuine, decades-old cardiovascular biology. Here's what the research actually covers.

The Basic Mechanism

Nitric oxide signals arterial smooth muscle to relax, widening blood vessels and reducing resistance to blood flow — a well-established piece of vascular physiology, not a novel or unproven claim.

What The Arginine And Citrulline Research Shows

Meta-analyses on both L-Arginine and L-Citrulline supplementation have examined their relationship to blood pressure outcomes, generally focused on vascular function and circulation rather than treating diagnosed hypertension.

An Important Distinction

This research concerns the individual amino acids studied at specific (often disclosed) doses — not the finished BloodPril product, which has not been the subject of its own clinical trial. That's a meaningful gap worth keeping in mind.
