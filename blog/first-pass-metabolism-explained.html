Why Most Arginine Never Reaches Your Blood

This is the single most important mechanical detail behind BloodPril's formula design — and the one most competing products never explain.

What First-Pass Metabolism Actually Means

When you swallow a capsule, its contents pass through your gut and then your liver before reaching general circulation. Both organs can break down a significant portion of oral L-Arginine before it ever reaches your bloodstream.

Why This Matters For Nitric Oxide Supplements

If the goal is raising blood arginine to feed nitric oxide production, and much of what you swallow gets broken down before it counts, you're paying for raw material that never does its job.

How L-Citrulline Solves It

L-Citrulline takes a different route — it's absorbed intact through the gut and converted to L-Arginine in the kidneys, entirely bypassing the gut-and-liver breakdown that limits arginine taken alone.

Why BloodPril Includes Both

BloodPril pairs direct L-Arginine (HCl and AAKG forms) with L-Citrulline Malate specifically to work around this limitation — see our full comparison piece for how the two actually differ.
