Chromium Picolinate and Insulin Sensitivity

Chromium Picolinate is the second ingredient in GLPro's formula, a trace mineral with a long research history in blood sugar supplements.

What is chromium?

Chromium is an essential trace mineral the body needs in small amounts, involved in how cells respond to insulin.

What research says

According to the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, chromium plays a role in glucose metabolism, though evidence for supplementation beyond preventing deficiency is mixed.
