Why Blood Sugar Swings Drain Your Energy

That familiar afternoon slump is often tied directly to how your blood sugar rises and falls throughout the day.

The rise and fall pattern

After eating, blood sugar rises and the body releases insulin to manage it. A sharp rise is often followed by a sharp fall — and that fall is frequently when the energy crash hits.

Why consistency matters

A pattern of repeated sharp swings, not any single meal, is what tends to leave people feeling drained over time.
