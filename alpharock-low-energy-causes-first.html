Low Energy: Find The Cause First

Low energy and low drive are symptoms, not a diagnosis.*

Common Causes

  • Poor or short sleep, and sleep apnea
  • Stress, low mood or depression
  • Diabetes, thyroid problems, anaemia or heart conditions
  • Medicines, alcohol and excess weight
  • Low testosterone, which needs a morning blood test to confirm
