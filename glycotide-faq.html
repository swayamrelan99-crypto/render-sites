Glycotide FAQ: Ingredients, Safety, Price and Refunds

Straight answers to what people ask most.

What is Glycotide?

Glycotide is a liquid-drop dietary supplement in a dropper bottle. The seller says it supports balanced blood sugar levels, healthy glucose metabolism, appetite balance and steady energy.* It is not a medicine and not a treatment for diabetes or any other condition.

What ingredients does it list?

The seller names five: gymnema leaf extract, chromium picolinate, panax ginseng, green tea leaf extract and coleus forskohlii root extract, and adds unnamed "additional plant extracts and amino acids". No amounts are given. See the ingredients.

How do I take it?

The seller says to follow the serving instructions printed on your bottle and use it consistently each day, without giving a serving size. See how Glycotide works and how to use it.

How long does a bottle last?

The seller says one bottle is a 30-day supply, 3 bottles are 90 days and 6 bottles are 180 days. Confirm the bottle size and serving on your own label.

Can I take it with diabetes medicine?

Only with your doctor's agreement. Gymnema, chromium and ginseng can lower blood sugar further, which could cause it to drop too low. See safety and medicine notes.

Will it lower my blood sugar or treat diabetes?

No. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, including diabetes. It never replaces medicine, monitoring or medical advice.

Will it stop sweet cravings?

No result is promised. Gymnema is traditionally linked to sweet taste, but evidence is limited, and cravings depend on sleep, stress, meals and habits.

Does it contain caffeine?

Green tea leaf extract usually carries caffeine. No amount is given, so be careful if you are sensitive.

Who should talk to a doctor first?

Anyone with diabetes or prediabetes, anyone on blood sugar, blood pressure or blood thinning medicine, anyone with a liver or heart condition, and anyone pregnant, nursing or under 18.

What does it cost?

The seller lists 2 bottles at $79 each ($158 plus $9.99 shipping), 3 bottles at $69 each ($207, free shipping) and 6 bottles at $49 each ($294, free shipping). See pricing and confirm every total at checkout.

How does the guarantee work?

It is a 60-day satisfaction guarantee. The seller recommends at least 30 days of use and says to contact its support team within the 60 days for refund assistance. See the 60-day guarantee and the refund policy.

Is this a one-time payment?

Yes. The seller says you pay once for the package you select, and you should review the total and shipping before you finalise the order.

Is Glycotide FDA approved?

No. The FDA does not approve dietary supplements, and this site shows no "FDA Approved" badge.

Why are there no star ratings or named customers here?

The seller shows named "verified purchase" reviews with staged photos and ratings, none of which can be checked, so this site shows plain themes only.
